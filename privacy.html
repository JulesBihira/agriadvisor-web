Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2025 · Last updated: 1 October 2025

AgriAdvisor ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of farmers and agricultural professionals who use our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the AgriAdvisor web app and Android app.

1. Information we collect

  • Phone number — used for OTP login and SMS weather/pest alerts (optional)
  • Email address — optional, used for account recovery and daily summaries
  • Farm profile — crops grown, farm size, region — used to personalise advice
  • Crop images — photos you upload for disease diagnosis; stored securely and used only for AI analysis
  • Location — used to show accurate weather and nearby market prices; we request permission and never track continuously
  • Device information — basic device type and operating system for app optimisation

2. How we use your information

  • Provide crop disease diagnosis and agricultural advice
  • Send SMS alerts for weather, pest outbreaks, and market price changes (with your consent)
  • Show localised market prices for your region
  • Improve our AI models using anonymised, aggregated data
  • Process subscription payments (we do not store card details — payments handled by MTN MoMo / Airtel Money / IremboPay)

3. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Google Cloud Vision API — for crop disease detection (image data only; not linked to your identity)
  • OpenWeatherMap — for weather data (location coordinates only)
  • FDI SMS Gateway / Africa's Talking — for SMS delivery (phone number only)
  • Law enforcement if required by applicable law

4. Data retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. Crop scan images are deleted after 90 days unless you choose to save them. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Your password is never stored — we use phone OTP authentication. Our backend is hosted on Railway.app with encrypted storage.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@agriadvisor.com or via the in-app settings page.

7. Children's privacy

AgriAdvisor is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via SMS or in-app notification.
