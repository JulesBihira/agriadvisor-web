About AgriAdvisor

Free farming app for East Africa

AgriAdvisor helps smallholder farmers in Rwanda, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Ethiopia find crop diseases early, sell at the right price and plant at the right time. It works on any smartphone, in English, Kinyarwanda, Kiswahili, Luganda and Amharic.

Crop Doctor

Take a photo of a sick leaf to find out what the disease or pest may be, and what to do next.

Market prices

Check crop prices at the main markets before you sell, starting with Kimironko, Nyabugogo and Musanze.

Weather for your farm

The forecast for your own district, so you know when rain is coming.

Planting calendar

When to plant and harvest your crops in each season.

In your language

English, Kinyarwanda, Kiswahili, Luganda or Amharic.

Your units

Farm size in acres in Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania, and in hectares in Rwanda and Ethiopia.

Questions

Is AgriAdvisor free?

Yes. Creating an account is free, and the free plan includes 3 Crop Doctor scans every month. Paid plans give you more scans when you need them.

How do I sign up?

Tap “Create one free” at the top of this page. Sign up with your email address – or, in Rwanda, with your phone number and an SMS code.

Which crops does it cover?

Maize, beans, potatoes, cassava, bananas and matooke, coffee, tea, rice, wheat, sorghum, teff, cashew and many more – the list changes with your country.

Do I need to download anything?

No. AgriAdvisor works in the browser of any Android phone or iPhone. Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

How can I contact AgriAdvisor?

Email hello@agriadvisor.co – for questions, partnerships or anything else.
