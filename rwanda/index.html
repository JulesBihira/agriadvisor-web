Questions

Is AgriAdvisor free?

Yes. Creating an account is free, and the free plan includes 3 Crop Doctor scans every month. Paid plans give you more scans when you need them. You can pay with MTN Mobile Money.

Can I use it in Kinyarwanda?

Yes. Choose Kinyarwanda when you create your account, or change the language later in Settings.

How do I sign up?

With your phone number (we send you an SMS code) or with your email address.

Do I need to download anything?

No download is needed. Open agriadvisor.co in the browser of any Android phone or iPhone, and add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

How is my farm size counted?

In hectares, like official figures in Rwanda. Small plots can be entered in square metres (m²).
