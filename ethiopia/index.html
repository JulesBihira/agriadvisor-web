Questions

Is AgriAdvisor free?

Yes. Creating an account is free, and the free plan includes 3 Crop Doctor scans every month. Paid plans give you more scans when you need them.

Can I use it in Amharic?

Yes. Choose Amharic when you create your account, or change the language later in Settings.

How do I sign up?

With your email address. We send you a link to confirm it, then you sign in.

Do I need to download anything?

No download is needed. Open agriadvisor.co in the browser of any Android phone or iPhone, and add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

How is my farm size counted?

In hectares, like official figures in Ethiopia.

How can I contact AgriAdvisor?

Email hello@agriadvisor.co. We welcome questions from farmers and partnership ideas from cooperatives, NGOs, input suppliers and buyers in Ethiopia.
